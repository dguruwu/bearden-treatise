# START HERE — a tribute to Thomas Bearden, and the fight he kept alive

This repository is a **tribute to Thomas E. Bearden** (US Army, retired), kept in the
discipline he deserved and rarely received: honest pricing. It is not affiliated with
Bearden, and it does not endorse his contested claims. It honors the fight.

## The debt

Around 2001 — twenty-five years before this repository was seeded — Bearden's papers
introduced its founder to a forgotten argument: that Maxwell's original [quaternion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quaternion)
formulation of electrodynamics was **truncated** in the 1890s by the vectorist reduction
(Heaviside, Gibbs), and that something was amputated in the process: the scalar
component, the longitudinal structure, the negative-signature directions of the algebra.
Without that introduction, this line of work would not exist.

## What is receipted here (computed, not recalled)

- Quaternion arithmetic: i² = j² = k² = ijk = −1.
- **A pure vector quaternion squares to a NEGATIVE REAL**: (0,1,2,3)² = −14.
  The quaternion algebra natively carries mixed signature.
- The [Gupta–Bleuler formalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gupta%E2%80%93Bleuler_formalism)
  (standard covariant photon quantization) puts the photon state space on an **indefinite
  metric**: "positive norms for spatial polarizations but negative norms for time-like
  polarizations." The scalar (time-like) photon is admitted in the formalism — and then
  projected out of physical observables. The physical Hilbert space is the quotient of
  the indefinite space by its zero-norm subspace. Mainstream physics, in other words,
  performs its own J-positivation on the photon — the amputation repeating inside the
  standard formalism.
