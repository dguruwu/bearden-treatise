## The debt

Around 2001 — twenty-five years before this repository was seeded — Bearden's papers
introduced its founder to a forgotten argument: that Maxwell's original quaternion
formulation of electrodynamics was **truncated** in the 1890s by the vectorist reduction
(Heaviside, Gibbs), and that something was amputated in the process: the scalar
component, the longitudinal structure, the negative-signature directions of the algebra.
Without that introduction, this line of work would not exist.

  The quaternion algebra natively carries mixed signature.
- The Gupta–Bleuler formalism (standard covariant photon quantization) puts the photon
  state space on an **indefinite metric**: "positive norms for spatial polarizations but
  negative norms for time-like polarizations." The scalar (time-like) photon is admitted
  in the formalism — and then projected out of physical observables. The physical Hilbert
  space is the quotient of the indefinite space by its zero-norm subspace.
- Mainstream physics, in other words, performs its own J-positivation on the photon —
  the amputation repeating inside the standard formalism.
