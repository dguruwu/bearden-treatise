# Provenance note

This repository grew out of a research session on 2026-10-28 (the scribe's raw log of
that day lives in the *other* repositories of this group; it is general provenance, not
Bearden-specific, and was removed here after review — the git history keeps it).
The Bearden-specific lineage is short and worth stating plainly:

- **c. 2001** — the founder first studied Bearden's papers (scalar waves, the truncated-
  Maxwell argument). Twenty-five years of carrying the question followed.
- **2026-10-28** — the question was cut: the quaternion mixed-signature receipts were
  computed, the Gupta–Bleuler indefinite-metric receipt was fetched, and the treatise
  (`the-amputated-signature-treatise-2026-10-28.md`) was drafted the same day.
- A tribute priced by the discipline in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
