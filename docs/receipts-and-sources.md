## Fetched quotes (verbatim, 2026-10-28)

**Gupta–Bleuler formalism** (standard covariant quantization of the photon):
> "this sesquilinear form gives positive norms for spatial polarizations but negative
> norms for time-like polarizations... a physical photon only has two transverse
> polarizations... the longitudinal component is merely an unphysical gauge... the
> physical Hilbert space [is] the quotient space of the three polarization subspace by
> its zero norm subspace. This space has a positive definite form, making it a true
> Hilbert space."

The time-like (scalar) photon: negative norm. The longitudinal photon: admitted, then
gauged away. The physical space: the positive-definite quotient of an indefinite space.
